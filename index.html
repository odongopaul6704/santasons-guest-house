About Us

A Home Away From Home in Lira City

Santasons Guest House is your trusted stopover along the Kampala Highway in Amuca Adyangopiro, Lira City West Division. Whether you're travelling for business, passing through, or simply looking for a comfortable retreat, we offer a warm, clean, and secure environment.

Our rooms are all self-contained with modern amenities, and our friendly team ensures your stay is nothing short of exceptional.

  • Conveniently located on Kampala Road
  • All rooms fully self-contained
  • Free WiFi & DSTV in every room
  • Secure parking & laundry services
